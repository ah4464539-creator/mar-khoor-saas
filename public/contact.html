QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

These answers cover the basics. Open AI chat for more guidance.

How do I get started with Mar-Khoor?

Choose your business type, create an account, add your menu or services, then configure the channels you need. Start registration.

Can I connect my existing WhatsApp number?

Mar-Khoor provides a WhatsApp connection flow. Availability and setup steps depend on your Meta Business account and number eligibility.

Does Mar-Khoor work for clinics, salons and hotels?

Yes. The platform supports business-specific tools for restaurants, clinics, salons and hotels. Individual integrations may require setup.

Where can I see plans and prices?

View the plans and plan builder. Final pricing depends on your country and selected services.

How do I request account or data deletion?

See the Privacy Policy for deletion instructions and email admin@mar-khoor.com with your request.
