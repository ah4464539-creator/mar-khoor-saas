Billing, cancellations and refunds

For business owners paying for a Mar-Khoor subscription or add-on. For a refund on a customer order or booking, contact the business that took your payment.

Open Plans & billing Contact billing support

Renewals

Your subscription renews monthly or yearly unless you cancel it. Before paying, check the amount, currency, billing frequency and any add-ons shown at checkout.

Cancel a subscription

  1. Sign in as the business owner and open Plans & billing.
  2. Choose Manage billing and cancel the subscription or add-on you no longer want.
  3. Check the confirmation for the date your paid access ends. Cancel before the next renewal to prevent the next charge.

When cancellation is scheduled for the end of your paid period, you can keep using the subscription until that date. If you paid manually or cannot open the billing portal, contact support from your account email before renewal.

Request a refund

Contact support if you were charged twice, charged in error or could not access a paid service as promised. Include your business name, account email, invoice number and the reason for your request.

We’ll review the charge and confirm the outcome by email. Cancelling a renewal does not automatically refund the current paid period. Your rights under applicable law still apply.
