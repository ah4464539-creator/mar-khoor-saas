    Questions

    Good to know

    Can I keep my own WhatsApp number?

    Yes. You connect your business number once from your dashboard, and the AI replies on it. You can read every chat and take over at any time.

    Which languages does the AI speak?

    It replies in the customer’s language, including Urdu, Roman Urdu, English, Arabic, Hindi, Turkish, Spanish, French and German.

    How do customers pay?

    You choose: cash, bank transfer, mobile wallets or a payment link. The AI shares your details, and you confirm the payment in your desk.

    What if the AI cannot answer?

    It passes the chat or call to your staff, and you can add your own notes so it answers the way you want.

    Do I need technical skills?

    No. Registration takes about five minutes, and setup guides you step by step.

    Ready for your first AI order or booking?

    Bring your orders, bookings and every customer channel into one workspace. Registration takes about five minutes.
