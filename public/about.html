ABOUT MAR-KHOOR

One front desk.
More time for people.

Mar-Khoor is an AI front desk platform for businesses that receive customer questions, orders and bookings. It brings website conversations, WhatsApp and AI phone assistance closer to one business workspace.

Explore Mar-Khoor ↗Contact our team

OUR PURPOSE

Give every business a more responsive front desk.

Answering the same questions, juggling multiple inboxes and manually recording bookings can take time away from serving customers. Mar-Khoor is designed to bring these interactions into a clearer workflow so teams can stay focused on their day-to-day business.

The goal is simple: help business owners manage customer conversations and follow-ups without needing a complicated collection of separate tools.

WHO IT'S FOR

Built for different kinds of front desks.

01

Restaurants & cafés

Help with menu questions, website orders, delivery or pickup workflows, and table bookings.

02

Clinics & doctors

Appointment enquiries, doctor-specific scheduling and reminders, with staff controlling bookings.

03

Salons & services

Service enquiries, available appointment slots, bookings and rescheduling.

04

Hotels & guest houses

Guest questions, room availability enquiries and reservation workflows.

HOW IT WORKS

Your customers can reach you through the channels they already use.

Each business chooses its setup. Features and third-party integrations become active only when configured and available for the business.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Helpful automation. Business owners stay in control.

AI can help answer routine questions and guide visitors, but it can make mistakes. Orders, bookings, payments and sensitive requests must follow the business's configured workflows and human oversight. Visitors should not share passwords, verification codes or sensitive payment details in public AI support chat.
