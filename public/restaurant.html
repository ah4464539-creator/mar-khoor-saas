    Payment methods

    💳

    How customers can pay you

    These options show at checkout on your website and in WhatsApp orders. Money goes straight to you. Mar-Khoor never holds it.

    Online card payments

    Customers pay by card on your website before the order is made. Payments go to your own Stripe account; Mar-Khoor never holds the money. Cash stays the default option.

    Bank & wallet accounts

    The customer sends money to this account and enters the transaction ID. Check your bank or wallet app, then press “Payment received” on the order.

    No accounts yet. Add JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or a bank account.
