Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Mar-Khoor handles information when you use its restaurant workspace, place an order on a restaurant website, or contact a restaurant through a connected WhatsApp or voice channel.

Information we handle

  • Restaurant workspace. Account email, password hash, restaurant details, team roles, menu information, sign-in sessions, and audit records.
  • Website orders. Contact and order details you supply, including ordered items and any notes you submit.
  • WhatsApp. The sender and business number identifiers, message IDs, message text, timestamps, replies, and delivery status used to operate the restaurant inbox and auto-replies.
  • Voice. When a restaurant enables an AI assistant, the call provider processes your speech. Mar-Khoor can receive call identifiers, status, end reason, and the transcript when the provider supplies it.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide accounts and restaurant services, route orders and messages to the right restaurant, generate requested website drafts and enabled AI replies, check delivery, troubleshoot problems, and secure the platform. The restaurant handles its own customer relationship and order fulfillment. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing can be based on providing a service you request, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, legal obligations, or consent when required.

Who receives information

Authorized staff at the relevant restaurant can see information needed to manage its orders, inbox, and calls. Hostinger hosts the application and database. Meta processes WhatsApp delivery and related events. When WhatsApp AI replies are enabled, Mar-Khoor sends the message text and relevant restaurant menu context to OpenAI to generate a reply. Website draft generation uses the restaurant details and description supplied for that purpose. Vapi and its configured providers process calls when voice is enabled. Providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Retention and sign-in

Retention depends on the record and why it is needed. We consider ongoing service needs, order and support history, security and audit requirements, and any legal obligations. Restaurant sign-in uses browser storage to keep the session active. Signing out removes that session from the browser.

Privacy and data deletion requests

To request access, correction, deletion, or another privacy right available under applicable law, email admin@mar-khoor.com. Include the restaurant name, the channel you used, and enough information for us to locate your records. For WhatsApp data deletion, use the subject “WhatsApp data deletion” and include your WhatsApp number with its country code. Keep passwords and verification codes private.

After verifying your request, we will delete the WhatsApp messages and associated identifiers and/or account data within 30 calendar days, including records held in Mar-Khoor systems where deletion is permitted. Deletion from third-party providers may be subject to their own processes and retention obligations. We will confirm completion or explain any lawful retention exceptions by email.

We may verify your identity and coordinate with the relevant restaurant. Some records may need to be retained where required by law. Where applicable, you may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority.
