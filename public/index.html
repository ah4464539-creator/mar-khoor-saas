    Questions owners ask us

    Can I keep my own WhatsApp number?

    Yes. Connect your business number once from the dashboard. You can read every chat and reply yourself at any time.

    Which languages does it speak?

    It replies in the customer’s language, including Urdu, Roman Urdu, English, Arabic, Hindi, Turkish, Spanish, French and German.

    How do customers pay?

    You choose: cash, bank transfer, mobile wallets or a payment link. The AI shares your details and you confirm the payment in your desk.

    What if the AI can’t answer?

    It hands the chat or call to your staff. You can also write notes, like parking or delivery areas, and it will use them.

    Do I need technical skills?

    No. Registration takes about five minutes and setup guides you one step at a time.

    Can my staff use it too?

    Yes. Add staff logins; they see orders and bookings, while settings and payments stay with you.
